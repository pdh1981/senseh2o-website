Privacy notice for data access accounts

This notice explains how we use the information you give us when you register for an account to access SenseH2O sensor data.

Last updated: [date of publication]

Who we are

SenseH2O is a research project funded by the Natural Environment Research Council (NERC). It is led by the University of Stirling, working with the University of Glasgow. The University of Stirling is the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

When you register, we ask for:

  • your name, email address and a username of your choice;
  • your organisation and the type of organisation;
  • how you intend to use the data, and, if you choose, a short description of your project.

We also record the date you accepted our terms of use, and keep basic sign-in records (when you signed in and the IP address you used) to keep the service secure.

If you ask for access to data from the wider Forth-ERA sensor network, we also keep a record of your request and our decision.

We will not use your details for marketing, and we will not sell or share them with anyone outside the project except as described below.

Our legal basis

We process this information because it is necessary for a task carried out in the public interest: the University's research and teaching, under Article 6(1)(e) of the UK GDPR. [to confirm with the University's Data Protection Officer]

Who can see it

  • Members of the SenseH2O project team at the University of Stirling and the University of Glasgow who manage data access.
  • The team that runs the sign-in and data servers, [name of the organisation that operates the SIEC servers], which stores the information on our behalf.

Your information is stored in the UK. [to confirm with the server operator]

How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as your account is active. When the SenseH2O project ends in June 2028, we will delete inactive accounts within [12 months], unless the data service continues, in which case we will tell you before anything changes. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to ask for it to be deleted. You can also object to how we use it. You can update most of your details yourself after signing in. For anything else, contact Forth-ERA@stir.ac.uk.

You can also contact the University of Stirling's Data Protection Officer at [DPO email address]. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
